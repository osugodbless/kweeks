# Demo Plan: Kweeks — full product tour (polished)

## What is this app?
Kweeks is a live money quiz: an instructor funds a real naira prize pool, players
join a room on their phones by a 4-letter code, everyone answers the same question
at the same second, and the fastest correct players split the pot — paid out to
their bank from their own screen.

## Goal
A 63-second polished product tour that shows the **whole** platform in detail, for
a hackathon judging panel and a projected venue demo. Every screen and the money
lifecycle (fund → escrow → podium → bank payout) must be visible.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a confident product film — "one wallet, one ledger, real
  naira out."
- Interpretation: elegant restraint. Mixed-case type, light-to-medium weight,
  generous spacing, one accent idea per scene. Slow dissolves between screens.
  The product's own copy does the talking; no jokes, no hype language.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 63s across 14 screens

## Visual identity
canvas `#faf3e7`, cream `#fffaf0`, ink `#24163f`, soft `#6b5f8d`, coral `#ff4d5f`
(action/live), violet `#6c4cf1` (host/selection), mint `#16c47f` (money/correct),
gold `#f6a91b` (win/prize). Fredoka display + Karla body. Dots texture, soft
blurred glows, hard card-3d shadows.

## Screens (the user flow, end to end)
1. **Hook** — "Quiz night. The prize is real money." + ₦50,000 pool.
2. **Host signup** — wallet issued at signup.
3. **Wallet dashboard** — stats + balance + create quiz.
4. **Fund wallet** — ₦50,000 credited instantly.
5. **Quiz builder** — deck, pool slider, winners, pacing, question editor.
6. **Live room** — projector preview, room code AB12, controls, roster.
7. **History** — the unified ledger (funding, pool, payouts).
8. **Player join** — code AB12, avatar, nickname.
9. **Lobby** — on the line ₦50,000, prize split.
10. **Question** — gold countdown, tap, mint "Correct!".
11. **Standings** — live board, "You" highlighted.
12. **Podium** — "You banked ₦25,000", claim code, redeem + confetti.
13. **Claim & payout** — bank account → "Paid out".
14. **Close** — the money rules + wordmark.

## Audio
- Role: warm, steady, premium bed with restrained motion-matched accents.
- Music: vol-12 (109.96 BPM, steady/clean — the polished bed), 63.6s, volume
  ~0.3, fade out under the close.
- Beat guidance: bundled preset (first 25s) + `hyperframes beats` for the full
  track; lock scene reveals to nearby strong beats where it does not hurt pacing.
- SFX: sparse; card/drop on reveals, one announcement hit on the podium payout,
  a soft logo hit on the close. Nothing on every beat.
- Audio-reactive: subtle — the money glow and ink cards breathe with bass/RMS.

## Transitions
Slow crossfade (0.5s) between every screen, over a shared paper background.
