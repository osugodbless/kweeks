## Problem Statement

An instructor-like person (teacher, office lead, family member) wants to run a live,
time-pressured quiz where participants compete in real time and the top finishers take
home real prize money. Today this does not exist as one flow: you can run live quizzes
(Kahoot-style) or you can pay people (transfer apps), but nobody joins them at the
quiz moment, using money as the stakes.

The hackathon deliverable must use BMONI's APIs/SDKs for every payment feature.
Exhibition: Friday 4 Sept 2026, 12:00 PM, Pier Place, Yaba, Lagos. 36 hours to build.

## What Makes This Cool

- **The live game is the whoa.** Many phones on one shared link, everyone sees the
  same question at the same second, a 30-60s timer counts down, answers land live,
  the leaderboard shifts in real time. Crowd energy carries the demo.
- **Money is the fintech frame.** The prize pool is a real wallet balance. At the
  podium, the top finishers are declared and each winner redeems from their current
  screen via BMONI. That is what a BMONI hackathon judge watches for: real API usage
  closing a real loop.
- **Instructor control as a product moment.** Auto-pace (autonomous) versus manual
  "I click next in the classroom" gives the demo a second beat most quiz tools lack.

## Constraints

- Solo builder, ~36 hours wall clock before the Friday exhibition.
- Must demonstrably use BMONI Embedded APIs (embedded-dev.bmoni.com sandbox, test keys).
- Demo runs live at a venue on phones over venue wifi. Reliability > feature count.
- Demo must work when audience joins with zero install (web only, no app download).
- BMONI lifecycle is heavy: a real payout to a student at minimum requires that
  student to be a BMONI user with an active wallet in the payout currency (exact KYC
  depth to be confirmed in the build-day-1 spike). True mass cashout is not a live
  demo moment.
- Full platform vision (auth, wallet deposits, KYC onboarding, bank cashout, org
  accounts, offices/families/schools) is a multi-month build. Out of scope for 36h.

## Premises

1. Web hybrid. Instructor and students in the browser. Supabase Postgres + Realtime
   syncs the game. BMONI money movement is server-side REST with test keys (owner key
   held server-side via create-managed; ethers/viem for signing). Flutter/RN wallet
   SDKs are the future mobile product, not this demo.
2. Students are lightweight players, not KYC'd wallet holders. Join = email + display
   nickname + chosen funny avatar. No identity docs, no wallet provisioning, no KYC.
3. Prize payout uses real BMONI sandbox calls where the loop can close: escrow wallet
   funded in sandbox, podium transfer executes against embedded-dev.bmoni.com. Mock or
   storyboarded screens are reserved strictly for what sandbox cannot do (per-student
   KYC cashout). This is what judges check first.
4. The live game is the product; money is the fintech proof. ~80% of effort to the
   synced quiz experience; payments are one vertical slice that closes the loop once.
5. (User addition, locked) Every player joins a quiz session with email, a display
   nickname, and picks from a set of funny random avatars. Email is the identity anchor
   that future cashout claims hang on.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Room-first MVP (CHOSEN)
  Summary: The synced quiz room IS the product. Real-time question sync, live
  leaderboard, auto or manual pacing, players join with email/nickname/avatar. BMONI
  is a genuine vertical slice: sandbox user for the instructor, wallet balance read as
  the pool, and one real sandbox transfer at the podium.
  Effort: M. Risk: Low-Med.
  Pros:
  - Protects the demo's whoa moment (live game) at a venue.
  - Shippable solo in 36h; real BMONI calls to point at.
  - Supabase Realtime + Postgres + auth is your stated stack; low new-tool risk.
  Cons:
  - Payout is single-transfer depth, not a payments platform. Judges who want
    full lifecycle must hear it in the pitch, not see it.
  - No deposits/webhooks/KYC; the "fund the pool" step is a sandbox credit, not
    a real fund-in demo.
  Reuses: Supabase (auth, realtime, pg), BMONI REST quickstart, create-managed wallets.

### Approach B: Fintech-first
  Summary: BMONI depth is the star: full sandbox instructor provisioning + KYC persona,
  virtual account deposit + webhook listener, prize ledger, batch payouts, transaction
  history screen. Game is a simpler single-screen poll.
  Effort: L/XL. Risk: High.
  Pros: Maximum visible API surface for a fintech judge.
  Cons: KYC sandbox is flaky and document upload is multipart + persona-matched; one
  mid-demo verification failure kills the show. Solo 36h on payment plumbing starves
  the game, and the "whoa" dies with it.
  Reuses: BMONI lifecycle docs end to end, Supabase functions + webhooks.

### Approach C: Custom realtime engine
  Summary: Skip Supabase Realtime. Small Node/WS server is both scoring authority and
  BMONI proxy. Full control of latency and anti-cheat.
  Effort: L. Risk: Med.
  Pros: Lowest-latency sync and server-authoritative scoring (matters when money is at
  stake later).
  Cons: You build the realtime layer from scratch in 36h solo; least reuse of your
  stated Supabase plan; venue wifi remains the real risk regardless of transport.
  Reuses: none meaningful. Discarded for time.

## Recommended Approach: A — Room-first MVP

Rationale mapped to constraints: solo, 36h, venue live demo, BMONI requirement.
A is the only option that protects the demo moment (the live game) while still placing
a real BMONI transfer in front of judges, and it runs entirely on the stack already
committed to (Supabase + web). B inverts the priorities toward what sandbox flakiness
can sink. C buys latency nobody measures at a hackathon while spending the scarcest
resource, your hours.

**Demo money event is multiple winners, winner-driven redemption.** The instructor
sets the winner count at quiz setup. At the podium the top N are declared with
amounts, and each winner's current screen offers redemption options immediately
(email not required first). One pre-provisioned persona closes the loop live so the
demo proves a real sandbox transfer to COMPLETED while the full claim set is created
and tracked. The pitch names this explicitly so judges see a real redemption model,
not a batch payout.

**Recipient rule is a build-day-1 spike, not an assumption.** The transfers doc says a
toUserId recipient needs an active smart wallet in the transfer currency and must be
your own user. Whether that also implies full KYC is unresolved against the real
sandbox. Provisioning the demo persona(s) on day 1 (with retry runway) and executing
one rehearsal transfer to COMPLETED settles both the rule and the demo proof before
the live show. If full KYC turns out to be required, the closing persona is a static
pre-provisioned fixture, not a live onboarding. The doc must not carry both answers.

### System shape

- **Client:** one responsive web app. Two surfaces: instructor dashboard (create quiz,
  set pool + winner count, pacing mode, start/next, live room control) and player room
  (join, answer, see leaderboard, podium redemption). One codebase, route-split. No
  install, runs on venue phones.
- **Backend:** Supabase. Postgres is the referee for the entire game: quizzes,
  questions, per-question durations, correct answers, started_at, answers, claims,
  redemption state. Realtime broadcasts room state and leaderboard deltas. Auth only
  for the instructor demo account. One Edge Function (Deno) holds the BMONI owner key
  and signs the money moves server-side.
- **Correct answers never leave Postgres.** Players receive questions and options
  only; scoring happens in a DB function against the stored key. No client ever sees
  the answer key, which is what makes the money trustworthy.

### Money (BMONI slice)
  1. Instructor "wallet" = a BMONI sandbox user with a CNGN smart wallet
     (create-managed, owner key held server-side in an Edge Function Secret). Balance
     is the displayed pool. Lock everything to NGN: credits, wallets, and transfers
     must all be NGN. A USD-funded wallet cannot pay an NGN pool.
  2. Funding the pool = sandbox test-credit (request NGN 1000 against your own phone)
     and/or a second-wallet transfer into escrow. Do not depend on a live balance
     fetch for the headline pool number; cache the last balance so the podium screen
     shows the pool even if the sandbox is slow at showtime.
  3. **Multiple winners, instructor-chosen.** At quiz setup the instructor sets the
     winner count. At the podium the top N players are declared, each with an
     allocated amount from the pool.
  4. **Winner-driven redemption, not instructor transfer.** Each winner's current
     screen shows redemption options immediately (bank transfer or the architecturally
     possible rails) with no email required first. Any redeem option clicked triggers
     a player claim tied to their join email, creates a redemption claim record, and
     opens the BMONI no-app/invite onboarding path for that email. An automated email
     also goes out as a recovery channel and proof artifact.
  5. Redemption state is tracked per winner: claimed -> invited -> onboarded -> paid
     (or failed). The platform keeps close track of whether each winner has redeemed.
     One pre-provisioned persona closes the loop live so the demo proves a real
     sandbox transfer reaching COMPLETED while N claims are created and tracked.

### Security model for money (revised — no privileged instructor fire button)
  The payout is not "instructor signs one transfer." It is a per-winner redemption
  claim. Guards:
  - **Per-winner claim_code.** When the podium resolves, the server issues each winner
    a claim_code delivered ONLY to their device/session, never broadcast. Redeeming
    requires the claim_code + matching email + participant id, checked by the Edge
    Function against the claim record. A rival watching the projector cannot front-run
    without the code. Claim auth is capability-based and honestly labeled as a claim
    token in the pitch, not over-sold as identity security.
  - Claim records are idempotent: one claim per winner per quiz; a double-tap or a
    replayed request cannot double-send.
  - The Edge Function that touches BMONI validates the claim_code and email against
    the winning participant's recorded row.
  - Money movement is server-side only (Edge Function secret), never in the client
    bundle.
  - The instructor has NO one-click "pay myself" path in the demo UI; the only way
    money moves is a winner redeeming their own claim, which is what judges watch.
  - Broadcasts and the projector never expose emails or claim_codes; standings show
    nicknames and participant ids only.

### Demo composition for the money beat (locked)
  - **Seeded demo room for money.** The money beat runs in a room the presenter seeds:
    5-8 phones, all the presenter's or helpers', with the persona device among them.
    Honest scoring runs; the presenter knows the deck and answers the persona to
    guarantee its podium seat. Judges watch the projection. A separate open room with
    no live payout (claims recorded, invites emailed, tracked) handles real audience
    foot traffic after the judged demo.
  - **Uniform terminal beat for every winner.** All winners see the same redemption
    flow: claim recorded, claim_code shown, "invite sent to your email — complete
    onboarding to receive payout". The persona's claim also completes live to
    COMPLETED. No winner sits in a permanent processing state; the audience sees a
    consistent story where one claim visibly lands.
  - **Settlement driver: one Edge Function does it all.** The redemption Edge Function
    validates claim_code + email, then runs the BMONI proposal -> approve -> sign ->
    send sequence for the invite/no-app path in a single invocation, updating claim
    state to paid on success. The screen polls or subscribes to claim state. No BMONI
    webhook into a dev host with no stable public URL; no DB-trigger queue layer a 36h
    demo does not need.

### Game authority (locked)
  - **Quiz authoring precedes the game.** Instructor authors all questions with
    correct answers, sets pacing (auto with per-question durations, or manual), and
    sets the winner count, before the quiz is saved and started. Correct answers are
    stored server-side at authoring time.
  - **Pacing advance:** AUTONOMOUS mode advances on a server-side schedule
    (question row duration + started_at checked by a scheduled function); the room
    does not depend on any client tab staying awake. MANUAL mode advances only on the
    instructor's next tap. Pacing mode is a flag on ONE room state machine.
  - **Answer referee:** every answer is a write through a Postgres RPC that stamps
    server now() and rejects if now() >= started_at + duration. Scoring is a DB
    function comparing against the stored answer key. The DB is the sole authority;
    a laggy or edited client cannot answer past the bell.
  - **Live leaderboard:** each accepted answer updates a server-side standings row;
    clients subscribe to the standings table via Realtime. Server owns the exact
    ordering; any client that drops self-heals by re-reading standings. No client
    recomputes standings from raw events.

### Player join (locked requirement)
Join screen collects email, display nickname, avatar choice from a funny avatar set.
Email is stored on the participant row; it is the anchor the future cashout claim
uses. No wallet, no KYC at join. In-room UX: prefilled email keyboard type, forgiving
validation, edits allowed before the first answer, single-tap avatar grid. The full
email-to-avatar join must fit ~15 seconds on a venue phone or the email decision is
revisited before demo day.

### Performance decisions (locked)
- **Per-answer standings writes are fine.** One upsert per accepted answer; at
  50-100 phones that is a handful of writes over 30-60s, each pushing a single row
  delta to subscribed clients. No throttling needed at demo scale.
- **One Realtime channel per room.** Room state, standings deltas, and presence share
  a single channel filtered on room_id. Well within Supabase limits at venue scale;
  no separate presence channel.
- **Optimistic claim, async settle.** The redeem tap writes the claim RPC and the
  screen immediately shows "claim created — processing". The BMONI handshake runs in
  the background Edge Function; the screen subscribes to claim state and flips to
  paid on completion. No spinner holds the money moment hostage to a multi-call BMONI
  flow. The instructor dashboard surfaces live transfer status as the fallback view.

### Code-quality decisions (locked)
- **Exactly-once claims:** claim creation is an idempotent Postgres RPC
  (SECURITY DEFINER) that inserts only if none exists for (winner_email, quiz_id),
  enforced by a partial unique index, and returns the existing row on conflict.
  Double-tap, replay, and duplicate submit collapse to one claim.
- **Email is recovery, never the critical path:** the automated redemption email goes
  out from an Edge Function after the claim RPC succeeds, with retry and a logged
  fallback to the instructor dashboard on provider error. On-screen redemption never
  waits on email delivery.
- **Deterministic tie-break:** podium ordering is correct-answer count, then
  cumulative answer speed (earlier correct answers win), then earliest join time.
  Computed in the scoring DB function so the podium is not arguable and the rule is
  one line in the pitch.
- **Stable participant identity:** the platform issues a participant id bound to the
  join email on the device; rejoin with the same email resumes the SAME participant
  (score and one claim slot preserved). One participant row per email per quiz;
  duplicate joins merge, never double-count.

### Room state machine + clock (the part that must not drift)
One room state machine; pacing mode only selects the advance trigger (AUTONOMOUS =
timer advances, MANUAL = instructor next tap). Clients render a countdown from a
broadcast `question_started_at` epoch; the server is the ONLY authority on answer
cutoff (now < started_at + duration), never client receipt, so jitter cannot desync
the room. Realtime broadcast delivers current question id + started_at; everyone
holds the same question and the same remaining time.

**Clock-skew correction (mandatory):** on join and on a light heartbeat, each client
measures offset = server_now - client_now (server stamps a time response). The
countdown renders from client_now + offset, so a venue phone whose clock lags 10-30s
still shows the true remaining time and stops exactly when the server would reject.
Laptop tests never catch skew; real drifted phones do, so the offset is measured, not
assumed.

Presence and rejoin (phones sleep, wifi drops, people arrive late):
- Lobby is held until the instructor starts; no question runs with no one in.
- Late joiners enter at the CURRENT question with zero backlog (say this in the pitch).
- Rejoin re-syncs to the authoritative question + remaining time from the server row.
- NoSleep (screen-wake) on player pages so the timer never sleeps a phone mid-question.
- Join link distribution: short join code + QR on the instructor's projected
  dashboard; players scan with the phone camera.

### Client stack + tests (locked)
- **Client:** Vite + React + TypeScript, @supabase/supabase-js. Chosen over Next.js and
  SvelteKit for the boring, well-trodden Supabase pairing and live-Realtime first-class
  support. Test plan artifact: godbless-eng-review-test-plan-20260903-170434.md.
- **Tests:** Supabase local (supabase start) runs the real Postgres + Edge Functions;
  integration tests exercise the actual RPCs and scoring SQL (late-answer rejection,
  exactly-once claims, tie-break determinism, redemption authz). Vitest for unit
  (tie-break comparator, pool allocation, countdown math). One Playwright E2E smoke of
  a full 2-player room + podium. Gate tests local, deterministic, <2s.

## Success Criteria

- Five or more phones join one quiz session at the venue and all see the same question
  simultaneously within ~1s, and the leaderboard updates live as answers land.
- Instructor can run both autonomous and manual pacing without a page reload.
- One real BMONI sandbox transfer executes at the podium (balance moves, status
  COMPLETED) driven by a winner redeeming their claim, to a pre-provisioned persona.
- Winners see redemption options on their current screen at the end of the game
  without opening email first; redemption state (claimed -> paid) is tracked and
  visible per winner.
- Rejoin mid-session works: a phone that drops and reconnects lands back on the
  current question with the correct remaining time, no backlog.
- A player joining late enters at the current question cleanly.
- Zero app installs for players; join is a link + QR in under 15 seconds on a phone,
  measured in the dry run.
- Everything runs on the machine/kiosk at the venue for the full demo without a crash.

## Next-Sprint (post-hackathon) — the scaled cashout path

The demo proves the redemption loop with one pre-provisioned persona closing live.
Post-hackathon, per-winner real payouts at scale run through the BMONI no-app
integration flow: the platform sends each winner an invite tied to their join email
(the identity the platform already holds), the winner onboards (KYC) inside the BMONI
app, and the platform reads the winner's account over the API to deliver the payout.
The demo builds the claim, on-screen redemption, email, and tracking machinery; the
product connects every claim to a real onboarded wallet.

  beat, seeded money room for the live transfer.
- Clock-skew: server-offset correction on join + heartbeat.
- Podium money UX: optimistic claim, async settle.
