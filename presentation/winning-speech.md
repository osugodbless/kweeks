# Kweeks Winning Speech

Full version (~3 minutes) and a 60-second emergency version. Read aloud.
Pause more than feels natural, especially after "somebody is about to get paid"
and "That's Kweeks."

---

## Full speech (~3 minutes)

**Open with a question. Pause. Let it sit.**

Somewhere in this room, somebody is about to get paid. Not next week. Not
after paperwork. Tonight.

**[Slow down.]**

Here's the thing about our world right now: we have more attention-grabbing
machines than any generation in history, and yet attention is the scarcest
thing we have. We scroll, we swipe, we watch, and we keep watching. But how
often do we truly lean in?

Look at a classroom. A trainer trying to hold forty phones and forty wandering
minds. Look at a quiz night, a training program, a boardroom that needs people
to actually care. Knowledge is everywhere. But attention is gone. And almost
every game we use to chase it ends in nothing. Points that evaporate. A
leaderboard that resets. Applause that buys nothing.

We think that's the wrong game.

Because money is the most honest attention meter ever invented. When real
value is on the line, people show up. They think faster. They remember longer.
They lean in. And in 2026, for the first time, money moves the way attention
should: instantly, programmatically, through the work BMONI has made possible.
A wallet in seconds. Rails that settle fast. A developer with a key can now
move value like a bank.

So we asked the obvious question. What if the quiz carried real money?

**[Pause.]**

That's Kweeks.

An instructor, a teacher, a host, a coach, funds a naira pool from their own
wallet. Players join with a four-letter code. No app. No account. No barrier.
Just a room. Same question, same second, everyone in it. Answer right, answer
fast, and when the quiz ends, the top players are paid. From the pool. Straight
to their own screen. In real time.

A scoreboard where the score is real. And the winner doesn't have to beg
anyone. The claim lands in their own hand, on their own phone, the moment they
win.

Tonight, on this stage, in a real environment, we watched the whole loop close.
A wallet issued. A pool funded. A winner paid. End to end, exactly the way it
will run when this stops being a demo and starts being everyday life.

**[Step forward.]**

Now, hear us on what we actually built. We did not build a trivia app. We built
a permission slip.

Permission for a lecturer who can't lose her classroom, because every correct
answer climbs a board that ends in real money. Permission for a training
manager who finally sees people finish the course. Permission for a campus, a
radio show, a sports bar, a family table on a Sunday night, where knowledge
becomes the sport and attention becomes the prize.

Our goal is simple. We want money to become a force for learning. We want every
classroom to feel like an arena. And we want winning to feel like winning
again.

That future doesn't exist without the people who made programmable money real.
BMONI gave us the rails. We brought the game. And when you put real value on a
real question, in front of a real room, something electric happens. People who
were scrolling are suddenly playing. People who were watching are suddenly
winning. People who never thought they'd earn a naira today are holding proof
that they did.

**[Pause. Slow, direct.]**

So when you leave here, don't remember Kweeks as a quiz.

Remember it as a promise. That learning can be electric again. That attention
can be earned, not stolen. And that the quietest person at the back of the room
can climb to the top of the money by being the smartest person in it.

We're just getting started. And the next player to join could be you.

Thank you.
