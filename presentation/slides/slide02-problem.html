The Problem

Quiz platforms and money apps exist separately. Nobody joins them together.

Quiz Platforms

Kahoot has 9B+ participants but zero real stakes. No money on the line means no real consequences for winning or losing.

Money Apps

Fintech apps handle transfers well, but there is no fun, no competition, no shared live experience. Sending money is a utility.

The Gap

You can run live quizzes or you can pay people, but nobody joins them at the quiz moment using money as the stakes.
